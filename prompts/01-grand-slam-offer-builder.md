# Grand Slam Offer Builder

Use this prompt to create a high-value offer using Hormozi's frameworks from the Pricing and Fast Cash playbooks.

## Prompt

```
Read the following playbooks before responding:
- playbooks/$100M Playbook_ Pricing -- Alex Hormozi -- $100M, 2025.md
- playbooks/$100M Playbook_ Fast Cash -- Alex Hormozi -- $100M, 2025.md

Now build me a Grand Slam Offer for my business:

**My business:** [describe what you sell]
**My target customer:** [who you serve]
**Current price point:** [what you charge now]
**Main result you deliver:** [the outcome your customers get]

Using Hormozi's Value Equation (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood / Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice), create:

1. The Dream Outcome statement for my offer
2. 3-5 ways to increase Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
3. 3-5 ways to decrease Time Delay
4. 3-5 ways to decrease Effort & Sacrifice
5. A list of bonuses that make the offer feel like a "stupid deal"
6. The recommended price point with justification
7. A guarantee structure that removes all risk from the buyer
8. The one-sentence offer statement I can use in ads and sales calls
```
