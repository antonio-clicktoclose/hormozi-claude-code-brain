# Lead Nurture Sequence Builder

Use this prompt to build a complete lead nurture sequence using Hormozi's Lead Nurture and Hooks playbooks.

## Prompt

```
Read the following playbooks before responding:
- playbooks/$100M Playbook_ Lead Nurture -- Alex Hormozi -- $100M, 2025.md
- playbooks/$100M Playbook_ Hooks -- Alex Hormozi -- $100M, 2025.md
- playbooks/$100M Playbook_ Marketing Machine -- Alex Hormozi -- $100M, 2025.md

Now build me a complete lead nurture sequence:

**My business:** [what you sell]
**My lead magnet / opt-in:** [what they signed up for]
**My core offer:** [what you want them to buy]
**Price point:** [your price]
**Typical buying timeline:** [how long from lead to sale]

Generate:

1. A 7-email nurture sequence with:
   - Subject line (using Hooks playbook principles)
   - Email body outline (2-3 paragraphs max)
   - CTA for each email
   - Send timing (day 1, day 2, etc.)

2. The content arc across all 7 emails:
   - Email 1: Deliver the lead magnet + set expectations
   - Email 2: Share a relevant story / case study
   - Email 3: Address the #1 objection
   - Email 4: Provide unexpected value
   - Email 5: Social proof / results
   - Email 6: Create urgency (without being sleazy)
   - Email 7: Direct ask + final CTA

3. 5 SMS/text message templates to pair with the email sequence
4. A re-engagement sequence (3 emails) for leads who went cold
```
